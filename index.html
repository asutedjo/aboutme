Armanto Sutedjo, Ph.D.

Educational Researcher & Learning Innovation Leader

I bring more than two decades of professional experience across education, instructional development, research, and organizational leadership, with a focus on how emerging technologies, particularly artificial intelligence, can advance teaching and learning in authentic educational settings.

My work centers on partnering with faculty, academic programs, and interdisciplinary research teams to design and implement evidence-based learning experiences, build AI literacy, and study the practical implications of generative AI for pedagogy, assessment, student engagement, and academic integrity.

I am especially interested in interdisciplinary collaborations that connect research, educational innovation, and professional practice to examine how AI can improve learning, support instructors, contribute to workforce development, and prepare students to become citizen-professionals who use emerging technologies responsibly, ethically, and effectively in their fields and communities.

Reciprocal Scholarly Framework

The Research–Teaching Nexus

My teaching and research are mutually reinforcing. I translate research on emerging technologies, learning, and educator readiness into authentic learning experiences, while questions emerging from teaching and interdisciplinary collaboration inform my research. This reciprocal relationship allows me to examine not only whether technologies such as AI can support learning, but how they can be designed and implemented responsibly in authentic educational contexts.
