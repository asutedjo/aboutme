Armanto Sutedjo, Ph.D.

Educational Researcher & Learning Systems Leader

I bring more than two decades of professional experience across education, instructional development, research, and organizational leadership, with a dedicated focus on how artificial intelligence can expand teaching and learning in authentic classroom settings.

My work centers on partnering with faculty, academic programs, and interdisciplinary research teams to design evidence-based learning experiences, support AI literacy, and study the practical implications of generative AI for pedagogy, assessment, student engagement, and academic integrity.

I am especially interested in interdisciplinary collaborations that examine AI as a tool for improving learning, supporting instructors, shaping future workforce development, and preparing students to become citizen-professionals who can use emerging technologies responsibly, ethically, and effectively in their fields and communities.
