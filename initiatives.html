Hear from Peers: Navigating AI in the Classroom

Initiative Overview: Founded and currently lead as Organizer and Host for this ongoing institutional webinar series (Fall 2023 – Present). The series creates a collaborative faculty community where educators share classroom-tested strategies, navigate pedagogical challenges, and exchange insights on integrating generative artificial intelligence into diverse disciplines.

AI Playground

Initiative Overview: Lead Facilitator for this hands-on workshop series (Fall 2023 – Present). The AI Playground provides faculty with a safe, experimental environment to test emerging generative tools, practice prompt engineering, and collaborate on building course-specific AI assets with direct pedagogical guidance.
